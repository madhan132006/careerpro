Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CareerPro AI.

Is CareerPro AI completely free to use?

Yes! This platform is developed as a final year project and is completely free for all students to use to evaluate and improve their career prospects.

How accurate is the AI Resume Analyzer?

We utilize Google Gemini 1.5 Flash to parse and score resumes against industry-standard ATS metrics. While highly accurate, we always recommend having a human review your resume as well.

What kind of data do you collect?

We only collect the academic and professional data you explicitly provide in your profile, skills, and resume. Your data is securely stored and never shared with third parties.
